Local Stakeholder Consultations (Online & Physical) for Bagerhat, Bangladesh under the Gold Standard Project Activity: “GREEN ICS BANGLADESH PART 3”

Dear Stakeholder,

We are inviting you to a Local Stakeholder Consultation (“LSC”) meeting for Physical and online its planned Gold Standard Project Activities in Bagerhat, Khulna, Bangladesh. This project will seek certification (VERs- Verified Emission Reduction) under the Gold Standard Project Activities “GREEN ICS BANGLADESH PART 3”. This initiative aims to distribute improved cookstoves in Bagerhat, Khulna, Bangladesh, to improve energy efficiency and reduce greenhouse gas emissions from the local atmosphere through joint collaboration by Green Spark Solutions Ltd., Green Spark Solutions Australia Pty. Ltd., and BKS Carbon Consulting Pvt. Ltd.

The details of the project activities for providing access to clean cooking technologies to end users and consumers in Bagerhat, Bangladesh are enclosed.

Publish Date: 24 May 2025

We are inviting all interested parties to attend one of the following Local Stakeholder Consultation meetings. Stakeholders who cannot physically attend have the possibility to participate online through Google Meet.
